Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind

Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind follows Joel Barish, a quiet man who discovers that his former girlfriend Clementine has erased her memories of their relationship. Heartbroken, Joel decides to undergo the same procedure, but as his memories begin disappearing, he realizes that he may not want to lose the memories of the person he once loved.
